# Website content provenance

This website is a static public description of Nexa. Content must preserve the distinction between accepted product scope, target technology, implementation evidence, acceptance, and production readiness.

## Product scope

The current Nexa Blueprint is the authority for product and domain decisions. Relevant sibling-repository sources include:

- `blueprint/01-shared/product/current-decisions.md`
- `blueprint/01-shared/product/owner-decisions-2026-09.md`
- `blueprint/01-shared/engineering/governance/publication-policy.md`

The accepted V1 is a multi-tenant B2B product for importers, distributors, and wholesalers, with a cold-chain focus. Product-scope acceptance does not certify implementation or production readiness. Public pages avoid asserting unverified endpoints, prices, SLAs, customer outcomes, and integrations.

## Team profiles and diagrams

Team names, profile areas, and portraits are sourced from the Nexa Mobile Report, including `report/01-presentation/1.1-startup-profile/1.1.2-team-member-profiles.md` and the related files under `report/assets/chapter-1/startup-profile/`.

The domain diagrams under `assets/img/domain/` are copied from the Mobile Report domain-storytelling artifacts. They represent target domain flows, not screenshots of an active Nexa instance.

## Publication boundary

`pages/about-the-team.html` and `pages/about-the-product.html` remain explicit placeholders pending publication review. The company page lists reported areas of contribution, not invented executive titles.
